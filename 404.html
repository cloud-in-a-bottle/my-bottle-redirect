my.cloudinabottle.org

This is a static page that redirects to your instance. It's used to enable things like OAuth flows that redirect back into your instance, or links that can be published with a common URL that will redirect into each user's individual instance. Only links under /redirect/… are forwarded, and they land under the same /redirect/… path in your instance — a reserved namespace of side-effect-free landing pages — so published links can never point at arbitrary pages in your instance.

Your instance URL will be saved in your browser's local storage. To forward a link, this page first asks your instance where its dashboard lives (a request to /.well-known/cloud-in-a-bottle on your instance, without cookies), since the owner can move it off the bare domain. It makes no other network requests.

Visiting my.cloudinabottle.org without any additional URL arguments will land you back on this page if you need to update your instance URL.
